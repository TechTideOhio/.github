# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

TechTide AI is committed to a professional, respectful, and harassment-free community. We welcome participation from people with different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives. This Code of Conduct applies to interactions in TechTide AI repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, focus criticism on ideas rather than people, respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences, and act in the best interests of the community. Give and receive feedback with professionalism, preserve confidentiality where appropriate, and assume good faith while remaining accountable for impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable. This standard applies whether the behavior occurs publicly or in private communications connected to the community.

## Reporting

Report a Code of Conduct concern privately through the contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md), with **"Code of Conduct report"** in the subject or message. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Include the repository or community space involved, a factual description of what occurred, relevant dates or links, and any immediate safety concern.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and may seek additional context from the parties involved. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern or participates in a review is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate to protect the community, including a private warning, removal of content, temporary restriction from a project space, or permanent exclusion from TechTide AI community spaces. Decisions are made based on the available information, the severity and pattern of conduct, and the safety of participants.

## Scope and Attribution

This policy applies to all TechTide AI project spaces and to behavior that materially affects the community. It is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), adapted for the organization’s needs.
